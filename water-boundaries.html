Reference correlations from IAPWS R14-08(2011). Horizontal axis: temperature (K). Vertical axis: pressure (Pa), logarithmic. Curves show ice–liquid coexistence and ice Ih–vapour sublimation, not all solid–solid phase boundaries.

Select a curve to inspect its reference correlation and valid temperature range.

These are numerical phase-boundary curves, not a complete phase classifier. Ice III and IX have different hydrogen-ordering structures; no ice IX stability field is inferred here. Ice XXI is a reported metastable form and is intentionally not plotted as an IAPWS equilibrium boundary.

Source: IAPWS R14-08(2011). The equations are reproduced as numerical correlations, with temperature validity limits enforced. A vapor–liquid saturation curve and solid–solid boundaries are outside this first calibrated pass.
