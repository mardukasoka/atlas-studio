Water · Ice Polymorphs & Phase Transitions

Explore water's molecular identity, stable high-pressure ice polymorphs, proton ordering and reported metastable forms. This overview uses labeled phase markers, not physical stability regions. Numerical phase boundaries will require a validated dataset.

Select a labeled region to inspect its structural identity and evidence status.
Ice III is a tetragonal, hydrogen-disordered crystalline phase; ice IX is its hydrogen-ordered relative. Ice XXI and other rapidly compressed forms require a separate kinetic/metastability layer rather than invented equilibrium fields. Water molecules remain H₂O across these polymorphs.

Water identity and transformations

H₂O molecular identity

Two hydrogen atoms covalently bound to oxygen. Bent molecular geometry; gas-phase bond angle approximately 104.5°.
